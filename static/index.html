TRAFFIC MEASUREMENT

Vehicle Counter

Count vehicles from traffic footage, and the measurement tools
that go with it. Runs entirely on your machine.

Vehicle Detector

Automated counting

Upload footage and a trained YOLO model, draw a region over each movement, and count. Results stream in as the video is processed — per-class totals, PCU-weighted flow and arrival intervals, exportable as CSV or JSON.

Tally

Manual counting

Hand-count vehicles in 15-minute HCM bins, by movement and by lane, with motorcycles separated. Use it to build the ground truth you validate the detector against.

Headway

Saturation flow

Lap-time a discharging queue to measure saturation headway and startup lost time at your own site, rather than adopting the HCM defaults of 2.00 s and 2.00 s.

Measure

Site geometry

Calibrate a scale on an overhead frame and measure real distances — lane widths, queue storage lengths, stop-line offsets.
Everything runs locally — no video leaves your machine. A vehicle is counted once, when its tracked centre first enters a region, and only after it has moved 60 px, so parked vehicles in view are not counted as traffic. Default confidence 0.20; counting windows advance by video time, never wall-clock.
