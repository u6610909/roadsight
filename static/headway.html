SP1 Saturation Headway

Discharge rate measurement — ABAC T-intersection
Method (HCM). Find a green phase with a standing queue. Tap once as each vehicle's front wheels cross the stop line, starting with the first vehicle when the light turns green.
The first 3–4 vehicles are slower — that is startup lost time. Saturation headway is the average gap from vehicle 4 onward.
Do 3–4 separate green phases, pressing "New cycle" between them. Use the lane with the longest queue (probably Bx in B_morning).
