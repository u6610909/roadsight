Pick the longest reference you can. Scale error divides by reference length, so calibrating on a 40 m lane marking is roughly ten times more accurate than calibrating on one 4.5 m car.

Good references, best first: a dashed lane line over many cycles (Thai standard 3 m mark + 9 m gap = 12 m period); a full zebra crossing width; a row of stopped cars bumper-to-bumper. A single car is the last resort.
